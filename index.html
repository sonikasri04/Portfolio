Research

Working paper · SSRN · Dec 2025
The Personalization Paradox: A Bias-Enabled Guidance Trap
↗

Examines how AI-driven career coaching systems, in optimizing for personalization through relevance weighting and reinforcement loops, can overfit to biased demographic patterns and narrow the aspirational paths shown to users.

Drawing on interviews with AI system architects, the paper identifies design mechanisms — diversity constraints, exploration parameters, transparency features — that balance algorithmic optimization against user autonomy, and proposes the Epistemic Immune System (EIS): a framework treating bias as a diagnostic signal rather than noise, using controlled bias exposure and structured exploration to surface hidden bias in AI-guided decision-making.
